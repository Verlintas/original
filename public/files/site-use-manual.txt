READING THE MARKS
-----------------
  ◯         A hollow circle, cut to the height of an uppercase letter.
            It marks a title. After the mark: one space, then the title.
  [ X ]     A text button. It downloads, opens, or goes somewhere.
  UPPERCASE Titles are shouted on purpose. It is a style, not anger.

VERIFYING A FILE
----------------
Where a file has a SHA-256 checksum, it is printed on the file's page.
On macOS or Linux, run:

  shasum -a 256 <file>

and compare. If the two strings differ, do not open the file.
