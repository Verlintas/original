DMCA TAKEDOWN NOTICE — TEMPLATE
===============================
Version 1.0 — 2026-10-17

TEMPLATE ONLY. This is not legal advice. The Digital Millennium Copyright
Act (17 U.S.C. §512) sets out specific requirements for notices and
counter-notices; a defective notice may be ineffective. Consult a qualified
lawyer before sending or acting on a notice. Misrepresentation in a notice
can create liability (17 U.S.C. §512(f)).

Use this file either as a template for sending a notice or as a basis for
a hosted takedown policy. Replace every value in [SQUARE BRACKETS].

---
PART A — NOTICE OF ALLEGED INFRINGEMENT
---------------------------------------
To: [DESIGNATED AGENT NAME]
[ORGANISATION NAME]
[POSTAL ADDRESS]
[EMAIL ADDRESS]

1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed:
   [DESCRIPTION, REGISTRATION NUMBER IF ANY, COPYRIGHT OWNER]

2. Identification of the allegedly infringing material and information
   reasonably sufficient to locate it (URL or precise location):
   [URL(S)]

3. Your contact information: [NAME, ADDRESS, TELEPHONE, EMAIL]

4. Statements required by 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3):
   "I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner
   complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or
   the law."

   "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that the information in this
   notification is accurate and that I am the copyright owner or am
   authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is
   allegedly infringed."

5. Signature: [PHYSICAL OR ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE]

Date: [DATE]

---
PART B — COUNTER-NOTIFICATION (IF YOU RECEIVED A TAKEDOWN)
----------------------------------------------------------
To: [DESIGNATED AGENT / SERVICE PROVIDER]

1. Identification of the material removed and its prior location: [URL]
2. Your name, address, telephone and email: [DETAILS]
3. Statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief
   the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.
4. Consent to the jurisdiction of [FEDERAL DISTRICT COURT / APPROPRIATE
   COURT] and agreement to accept service from the complainant.
5. Signature and date.

PART C — HOSTED TAKEDOWN POLICY SKELETON
----------------------------------------
- Designated agent: [NAME, ADDRESS, EMAIL] (register with the U.S.
  Copyright Office where required).
- Takedown process: acknowledge receipt, review completeness, remove or
  disable access, notify the uploader, allow counter-notice, restore where
  the statutory conditions are met.
- Repeat infringers: [TERMINATION POLICY].
- Records: retain notices and actions for [PERIOD].
- Misuse: warn that knowingly false notices may create liability.
