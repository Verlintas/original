1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy sets out what you may not do when using [SERVICE]. It applies
to all users and to all content submitted through the service.

2. PROHIBITED CONTENT
---------------------
You must not upload, post or share content that:
- is unlawful, defamatory, fraudulent or deceptive;
- infringes copyright, trade marks, privacy or other rights;
- is harassing, threatening, hateful or discriminatory;
- is sexually explicit involving minors, or otherwise exploitative;
- contains malware, spyware or code designed to harm;
- discloses personal data of others without a lawful basis.

3. PROHIBITED ACTIVITY
----------------------
You must not:
- access systems or data without authorisation;
- probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the service without permission;
- bypass rate limits, authentication or access controls;
- interfere with the service, including denial-of-service attempts;
- use automated means to scrape or overload the service contrary to
  [ROBOTS.TXT / TERMS];
- impersonate others or misrepresent affiliation;
- use the service to send spam or unlawful communications.

--------------------
We welcome good-faith security research under [SECURITY POLICY LINK /

violation of this policy.
