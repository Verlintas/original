# AI Contribution Policy — [PROJECT NAME]

## PURPOSE

This policy explains how AI-assisted contributions are handled in
[PROJECT NAME]. The goal is to keep the project's licensing clear, its
quality high, and responsibility with humans.

## SCOPE

This policy covers code, documentation, tests, translations, designs, and
issue or pull request text produced in whole or in part with generative AI
tools (assistants, code generators, chat models, agents).

## DISCLOSURE

- Disclose substantial AI assistance in the pull request description:
  which tool was used and for what (for example, drafting tests or
  refactoring).
- Routine assistance (autocomplete, formatting) does not need disclosure.
- Do not include AI-generated content that you cannot license under the
  project's licence ([LICENSE IDENTIFIER]).

## HUMAN RESPONSIBILITY

- A human contributor is responsible for every line of a contribution,
  including AI-generated code.
- Contributors must understand the change, be able to explain it in review,
  and maintain it afterwards.
- "The model wrote it" is not an acceptable answer to a review question.

## LICENSING AND PROVENANCE

- Verify that AI output does not reproduce substantial portions of
  third-party code with incompatible terms.
- Do not paste confidential, proprietary, or personal data into external AI
  services.
- Sign off commits under the DCO, or follow the CLA where applicable.
- Keep prompt-and-output records where required by [POLICY / REGULATION].

## QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

AI-assisted contributions follow the same rules as any other change:
- tests for behaviour changes;
- documentation updates;
- no unreviewed generated code in security-sensitive areas;
- code style and licensing checks pass.

## PROHIBITED USES

- Generating malicious code, obfuscation, or evasion techniques.
- Submitting generated vulnerability reports without validation.
- Impersonating a human reviewer or fabricating test results.
- Using AI to mass-produce low-quality issues or pull requests.

## AGENTS AND AUTOMATION

Automated agents may interact with the repository only through
[APPROVED CHANNELS] and must identify themselves as automated. Agents do
not have merge rights; a maintainer reviews and merges.

## ENFORCEMENT

Maintainers may request changes, close contributions, or restrict
participation for violations. Repeated or deliberate violations may lead
to a ban under the [CODE OF CONDUCT].

The policy is reviewed every [6/12] months. Changes follow the project's
governance process: [GOVERNANCE LINK].
