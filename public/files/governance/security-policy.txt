## REPORTING A VULNERABILITY

Please report security issues privately:

- Email: [SECURITY EMAIL] (PGP: [KEY FINGERPRINT / LINK])
- Or use GitHub private vulnerability reporting: [REPO SECURITY TAB LINK]

Do not open a public issue, discussion, or pull request for a vulnerability.

Include, where possible:
- affected version and platform;
- a description of the issue and its impact;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- any known mitigations;
- how you would like to be credited.

## WHAT TO EXPECT

- Acknowledgement within [3] business days.
- Triage and severity assessment within [7] business days.
- A fix timeline depends on severity; we aim for [30/90] days for critical
  issues.
- We will keep you informed and coordinate disclosure with you.

## COORDINATED DISCLOSURE

We follow coordinated disclosure. We ask that you give us [90] days before
public disclosure, or less if a fix ships sooner. We will credit reporters
in the advisory unless you prefer to remain anonymous.

## SAFE HARBOUR

We consider good-faith security research authorised when it:
- stays within [SCOPE: PRODUCTION SYSTEMS, TEST ACCOUNTS];
- avoids privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption;
- uses only the minimum access needed to demonstrate the issue;
- is reported promptly and kept confidential until coordinated disclosure.

We will not pursue legal action for research that follows this policy.

## OUT OF SCOPE

- [SOCIAL ENGINEERING, PHYSICAL ATTACKS, DENIAL OF SERVICE]
- Issues in third-party dependencies that are already publicly known and
  fixed upstream; report those upstream first.
- [OTHER EXCLUSIONS]
