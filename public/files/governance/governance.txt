# [PROJECT NAME] — Governance

## SCOPE

This document explains who makes decisions in the project, how those
decisions are made, and how roles change. It applies to the repository, its
releases, and the community spaces listed in [COMMUNITY SPACES].

## ROLES

**Users** — use the software and report problems.

**Contributors** — submit issues, documentation, code, translations, or
design. Any contributor who has had [N] substantial changes merged in the
last [12] months is listed in [CONTRIBUTORS FILE].

**Reviewers** — trusted contributors who review and approve pull requests.
Nominated by a maintainer and confirmed by lazy consensus of maintainers.

**Maintainers** — responsible for direction, releases, access control, and
the health of the community. Current list: [MAINTAINERS FILE].

## DECISIONS

- **Lazy consensus** is the default: a proposal is accepted if no maintainer
  objects within [7] days.
- **Discussion first** for large changes: open an issue or RFC in
  [RFC LOCATION] and allow [14] days for comment.
- **Voting** is reserved for exceptional cases: a simple majority of
  maintainers, with ties broken by [TIE-BREAK RULE].
- Decisions and their rationale are recorded in [DECISION LOG].

## CHANGING MAINTAINERS

- Adding a maintainer requires nomination by an existing maintainer and
  confirmation by [TWO-THIRDS] of maintainers.
- A maintainer may step down at any time.
- Inactive maintainers (no reviews or commits for [6] months, after a
  check-in) may be moved to emeritus status by maintainer consensus.

## RELEASES

Release authority and cadence are defined in [RELEASE POLICY]. Only
maintainers may publish releases.

## CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

Maintainers must disclose relevant commercial or personal interests before
decisions that could benefit them materially. Affected maintainers
[RECUSE THEMSELVES / DISCLOSE AND PARTICIPATE].

## CODE OF CONDUCT

Participation is governed by the [CODE OF CONDUCT]. Enforcement is handled
by [ENFORCEMENT TEAM] as described there.

## FORKS AND DERIVATIVES

and not imply endorsement. Trademark rules: [TRADEMARK POLICY].

## AMENDMENTS

Changes to this document require a pull request, a [14]-day comment period,
