The first version is live. This note records what it includes, and — just as honestly —
what it does not.

## WHAT VERSION 1.0 INCLUDES

- A home page, a **FILES** section with detail pages, **NOTES**, **LINKS**, **ABOUT**,
  a **COLOPHON**, a custom 404 page, and an RSS feed.
- Small files served from the site itself, large files pointed at GitHub Releases.
- Metadata on every file: date, category, version, format, size, licence, checksum.

## WHAT IS DELIBERATELY MISSING

- Search. With this many files, a list is faster than a search box.
- Comments and accounts. Nothing here needs either.
- Dark mode. The site is white by definition, not by preference.

## WHAT MAY COME NEXT

More files, more notes, and whatever the next honest need turns out to be. Nothing is
added because it is expected; things are added because they are missing.
