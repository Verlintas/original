Original is a plain place. White background, red text, and nothing else — no banners,
no images that move, no cookies asking to be accepted. It exists to hold files, show
them, and hand them out.

## WHY A SITE AT ALL

Files deserve an address. A folder on a disk is a drawer; an address is a door. This
site is the door: everything here can be linked to, printed, archived, and found again.

## WHY SO PLAIN

Because the files are the content. A site that stores and distributes documents should
not compete with them. Red on white is enough contrast to read and enough restraint to
stay out of the way.

## WHAT COMES NEXT

The structure is deliberately larger than the current contents. Sections will appear
as they fill: more notes, more formats, more of whatever turns out to be worth keeping.
Everything is versioned, so nothing quietly disappears.
