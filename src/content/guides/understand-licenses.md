Most licenses look longer than they are. Read them in three passes.

## 1. PERMISSIONS

What may you do?

- commercial use
- modification
- distribution
- private use
- patent use

If a permission is missing, do not assume it is allowed.

## 2. CONDITIONS

What must you do in exchange?

- include the copyright notice
- state changes
- disclose source
- use the same license
- include a NOTICE file

These are the obligations that actually affect how you ship the work.

## 3. LIMITATIONS

What does the license refuse to promise?

- no warranty
- no liability
- no trademark rights

Almost every license disclaims warranty and liability. That does not mean the software is safe; it means the author is not promising that it is.

## A QUICK READING METHOD

1. Find the grant of rights.
2. Find the conditions attached to redistribution.
3. Find the warranty and liability paragraphs.
4. Find the termination clause.
5. Check the SPDX identifier and the full text.
