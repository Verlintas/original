An accessibility statement is a public promise plus a feedback channel. It should be specific, honest, and dated.

## INCLUDE

- the site or product name
- the target standard, for example WCAG 2.2 Level AA
- what has been tested
- known limitations
- the date of the last review
- how to report a barrier
- what happens after a report
- who is responsible for accessibility

## DO NOT CLAIM

- full conformance without testing
- testing with every assistive technology
- that an automated checker proves accessibility

Accessibility is a practice, not a badge.

## A USEFUL STRUCTURE

1. **Commitment:** what the site is trying to achieve.
2. **Measures:** semantic HTML, keyboard support, contrast, zoom, reduced motion.
3. **Limitations:** known gaps and third-party content.
4. **Feedback:** email, response target, and what to include.
5. **Review date:** when the statement was last checked.
