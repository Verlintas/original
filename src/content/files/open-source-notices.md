A template for third-party notices in a distributed product: component index, license texts, and the requirements that specific licenses impose.

## USE

- List every third-party component with its version and license identifier.
- Reproduce required notices and license texts.
- Generate the inventory with a scanner, then review it manually.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Apache-2.0 NOTICE files and LGPL/GPL source offers are the usual failure points.
