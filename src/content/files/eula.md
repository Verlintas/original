A starting point for an end-user license agreement: license grant, restrictions, store terms, ownership, updates, privacy, warranty, liability and termination.

## USE

- Fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and match the distribution channel.
- Add the store's required clauses where applicable.
- If the software is open source, a EULA is usually unnecessary — check the licence first.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Apple and Google impose additional requirements for apps distributed through their stores.
