The per-file notice for projects licensed under MIT: a copyright line and
an SPDX identifier, plus notes on comment syntax for common languages.

## USE

- Paste the two lines at the top of each source file.
- Keep the full license text in the project's `LICENSE` file.
- Replace `<year>` and `<copyright holders>` with your details.

## NOTES

- The SPDX line is optional but recommended; scanners understand it.
- The header file includes comment-syntax examples for C-family languages,
  shell, Python, HTML and SQL.
