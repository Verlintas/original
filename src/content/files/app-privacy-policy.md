A starting point for an Android app privacy policy: data collected, runtime
permissions, third-party SDKs, retention, your choices, deletion, and
children — with placeholders throughout.

## USE

- Fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]`; list each third-party SDK and its policy.
- Keep the published policy consistent with the Play Console data-safety
  declarations.
- Have a lawyer review it before publishing.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Written with Google Play's privacy-policy and data-safety requirements in
  mind; the deletion path and contact details are mandatory in practice.
