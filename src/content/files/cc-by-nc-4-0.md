The complete legal code of the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International license. The human-readable deed is a summary; this is the text that actually grants the rights.

## USE

- Use for creative works such as text, images, audio, video, and data.
- Choose the variant that matches the rights you want to grant.
- Give attribution, link to the license, and indicate changes as required.

## NOTES

- License: CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Creative Commons recommends against using CC licenses for software.
- This is the legal code, not the human-readable deed.
