The per-file notice for libraries released under LGPLv3, following the wording recommended by the GNU project.

## USE

- Paste the notice at the top of each library source file.
- Replace the library name, year and author placeholders.
- Remove the "or later" sentence to license under LGPL-3.0-only.

## NOTES

- Keep both the LGPL and GPL texts with the project; the LGPL incorporates GPLv3 by reference.
- SPDX identifiers: `LGPL-3.0-or-later` or `LGPL-3.0-only`.
