A lightweight convention on top of commit messages: type, optional scope, description, body, and footers. It makes history readable and enables automated changelogs and semantic versioning.

## USE

- Start the subject with a type such as feat, fix, docs, or chore.
- Use BREAKING CHANGE: in the footer for incompatible changes.
- Keep the subject imperative and concise.

## NOTES

- The specification is licensed CC BY 3.0.
- Pairs well with SemVer and Keep a Changelog.
