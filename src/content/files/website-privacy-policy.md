A starting point for a website privacy policy: who you are, what you
collect, legal bases, sharing, transfers, retention, rights, security and
cookies — with bracketed placeholders for your details.

## USE

- Fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and delete sections that do not apply.
- Keep the final policy consistent with what the site actually does.
- Have a lawyer review it before publishing.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Structure follows GDPR Articles 13–14 and common US state privacy notice
  requirements.
- The CCPA/CPRA section can be removed if you have no California users.
