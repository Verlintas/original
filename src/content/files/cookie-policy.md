A starting point for a cookie policy: what cookies are, the four usual categories, consent under EU/UK rules, and how to manage them.

## USE

- Fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and delete categories you do not use.
- Keep the cookie table consistent with what the site actually sets.
- Pair non-essential cookies with a consent tool where required.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Written with the ICO cookies guidance and the ePrivacy Directive in mind.
