A starting point for an individual CLA: copyright and patent grants, representations, retention of ownership, and a signature block.

## USE

- Have a qualified lawyer adapt it before use.
- Consider the DCO instead: it is lighter and sufficient for many projects.
- Keep signed agreements and record them in [CLA RECORDS].

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- A CLA must be consistent with the project licence and local law.
