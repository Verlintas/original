The Developer Certificate of Origin, version 1.1, verbatim. A per-commit statement that the contributor has the right to submit the work under the project licence.

## USE

- Require `git commit -s` in CONTRIBUTING; the sign-off line is the certification.
- Check sign-offs in CI or with a bot for every pull request.
- Keep this file unchanged; the certificate only works verbatim.

## NOTES

- The DCO text may be copied and distributed verbatim; it may not be modified.
- Many projects use the DCO instead of a CLA.
