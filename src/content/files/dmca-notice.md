Templates for a DMCA takedown notice and counter-notice under 17 U.S.C. §512, plus a skeleton for a hosted takedown policy.

## USE

- Fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and remove the parts you do not need.
- Follow the statutory statements exactly; defective notices may be ineffective.
- Register a designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office where required.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Knowingly false notices can create liability under §512(f).
