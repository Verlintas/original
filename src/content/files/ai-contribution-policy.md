A modern contribution policy for AI-assisted work: disclosure thresholds, human responsibility for every line, licensing and provenance checks, quality rules, and agent behaviour.

## USE

- Adapt the disclosure threshold to the project culture.
- Keep the licensing section aligned with the project licence.
- Review the policy at least every six months.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Requirements for provenance records vary by jurisdiction and contract.
