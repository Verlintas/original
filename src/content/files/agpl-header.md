The per-file notice for programs released under AGPLv3, adapted from the GNU how-to.

## USE

- Paste the notice at the top of each source file.
- Replace the program name, year and author placeholders.
- Offer the corresponding source to users who interact with the program over a network.

## NOTES

- The AGPL adds the Section 13 network condition to GPLv3.
- SPDX identifiers: `AGPL-3.0-or-later` or `AGPL-3.0-only`.
