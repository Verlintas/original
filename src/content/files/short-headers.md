One-line SPDX headers for licenses that do not publish their own per-file notice. Use the identifier with a copyright line; keep the full license text in the project root.

## USE

- Pick the identifier for your license and add the copyright line.
- Keep the corresponding full text under `LICENSES/` or `LICENSE`.
- For CC0 and Unlicense, attribution is optional but welcome.

## NOTES

- SPDX identifiers are understood by license scanners and package metadata.
- The REUSE specification builds on per-file SPDX headers.
