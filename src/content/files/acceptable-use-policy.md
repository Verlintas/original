A template for the rules of a service: prohibited content, prohibited activity, security research safe harbour, enforcement, reporting and appeals.

## USE

- Fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]` and adapt the rules to the service.
- Link it from the terms of service and the sign-up flow.
- Define a clear abuse-reporting address.

## NOTES

- **This is a template, not legal advice.**
- Keep enforcement proportionate and document decisions where practicable.
